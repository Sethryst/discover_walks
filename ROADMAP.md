# Gremlin Lab roadmap

This roadmap describes the project in chapters rather than promising fixed dates. The repository’s GitHub Project is the operational task board; this file is the durable narrative map.

## Chapter 1 — Field journal foundation

- Keep Walk & Wildlife useful as a local-first walking journal.
- Preserve private-by-default walks, observations, reflections, and media.
- Make the core experience understandable without a hosted account.

Suggested milestone: `Field Journal v1`

## Chapter 2 — Living regional atlas

- Publish trustworthy regional places, trails, civic information, stories, and audio.
- Keep source provenance and attribution attached to published artifacts.
- Improve regional discovery without implying unsupported certainty.

Suggested milestone: `Regional Atlas`

## Chapter 3 — Verified walking intelligence

- Expand pedestrian routing through validated walking cells.
- Preserve stable graph identity, edge names, checksums, and source releases.
- Report missing or failed routes honestly.

Suggested milestone: `Routing Beta`

## Chapter 4 — National synthesis

- Expand coverage through resumable, gated regional conversion.
- Connect geographic, civic, historical, and natural-world layers.
- Treat the result as a living geospatial atlas rather than a generic points-of-interest directory.

Suggested milestone: `National Atlas Preview`

## GitHub Project views

Use a small number of views so the board remains legible:

1. **Roadmap** — all open work grouped by milestone.
2. **Now** — items actively being researched, built, or verified.
3. **Data and routing** — source, geography, graph, and publication work.
4. **Release readiness** — tests, attribution, documentation, and deployment gates.

Recommended statuses: `Backlog`, `Research`, `Building`, `Verifying`, `Published`, and `Parked`.
