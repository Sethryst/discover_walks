# Appalachian Corridor Lab POI family policy · 2026-08-20

Status: research-grade; review-only.

## Allowed

- Official parking and signed trailheads.
- Official vistas, shelters, and campsites inside the research envelope.
- Official water sources only when a maintained authoritative layer exists.
- Café or store candidates only after an entry is editor-verified, within 1 mile of that entry, and on a documented logical approach.

## Excluded

- Generic neighbourhoods and distant towns.
- OSM-only parking as entry evidence.
- Businesses measured only to the lab center.
- Any point with unknown geometry, provenance, or current access.
