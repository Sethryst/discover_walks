Discover Walks

Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

Discover Walks is local-first. Walk routes, journal entries, observations, photos, and precise location history stay in your browser unless a future feature clearly asks you to share them. Optional accounts currently sync only the information described below.

Who operates this service

Discover Walks is an independent Gremlin Labs project. Questions, privacy requests, and account-deletion requests can be sent to wellnesswalkshamptonroads@gmail.com.

Information processed on your device

The app may store regional preferences, favorite places and categories, accessibility preferences, walks, route coordinates, journal reflections, nature observations, photos, points, and app settings in browser storage. Browser geolocation is used only after permission. This local information is not uploaded by the normal journal, walk, or observation features.

Clearing this site’s browser storage or using the app’s deletion controls removes local records from that browser. Browser or device backups may retain copies according to their own settings.

Optional account and Google sign-in

You can use the core local app without an account. If you choose Google sign-in, Google authenticates you through Supabase Auth. The requested scopes are limited to OpenID identity, email address, and basic profile information. The app does not receive your Google password and does not request access to Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive, or other Google content.

Supabase may store your account identifier, email, authentication-provider metadata, and session information. Discover Walks does not use Google provider tokens to call other Google APIs and does not intentionally store Google access or refresh tokens in its public application database.

Minimal project heartbeat

When the app is opened and Supabase is configured, it may make a small row-level-security-protected database request no more than twice per day. It downloads no profile rows and is used only to verify service availability. Your browser stores the last attempt time locally.

Maps, conditions, and external services

GitHub Pages delivers the website and receives ordinary web-request information such as IP address and browser headers under GitHub’s practices. Map tile providers receive requests for the map area you view and may infer an approximate viewed area from those requests. Live weather and daylight requests use the selected region’s center by default rather than your precise GPS position. External official links open separately and are governed by the destination’s privacy policy.

The app currently uses or may contact Supabase, Google authentication, GitHub Pages, OpenStreetMap tile infrastructure, the National Weather Service, Open-Meteo, Sunrise-Sunset, and official government/open-data sources. These providers may process IP addresses and request metadata needed to deliver their services.

Why information is used

Information is used to operate requested app features, authenticate accounts, synchronize optional aggregate statistics, connect accepted friends and cohorts, protect access with row-level database rules, troubleshoot service failures, and comply with legal obligations. Discover Walks does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it or clear browser storage. Online account and community data remains until deleted, the account is removed, or retention is required for security or legal reasons. To request deletion of your online account and associated application data, email the address above from the account email. We may need to verify ownership.

Security and choices

Public browser code uses a Supabase publishable key; database Row Level Security is the authorization boundary. No system is perfectly secure. You can avoid online processing by remaining in local mode, deny location permission, remove site storage, revoke Google access in your Google Account, or request account deletion.

Children

The online service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided information should contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy as features or providers change. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date before the changed processing is relied upon where required.
