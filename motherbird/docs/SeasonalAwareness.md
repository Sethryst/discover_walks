# Seasonal Awareness Contract

A place has rhythms as well as coordinates. Field Edition intelligence contributes a bounded, source-backed walking experience for a place and time; it does not expose raw provider records as map pins.

Every specialist must emit `createSeasonalAwareness()` input with a place, why it matters now (`seasonalReason`, optional `weatherCondition`, `timeWindow`), one or more walker actions, confidence, collection time, and a source trail including license/provenance.

The foundational specialists are `time`, `weather`, and `habitat`. Wildlife, trees, history, and art consume this shared calendar/context layer rather than maintaining independent season logic. The approved action vocabulary is `look`, `listen`, `rest`, `explore`, and `take_a_detour`; confidence is `confirmed`, `likely`, or `possible`.

Specialists may only use approved sources and must preserve source URL, collection timestamp, and license. Editorial review decides what becomes visible in a Field Edition.
