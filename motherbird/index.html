      DESIGNED FOR YOUR DEVICE

      Private by default.

      • Walks, reflections, and observations are stored in this browser using IndexedDB.
      • Location is only requested while you choose to use it. This web demo does not track in the background.
      • Map tiles are cached as you view them; open the app once while online before relying on a route offline.
      • Historic prompts use a small prototype dataset. Confirm accessibility, hours, and safety guidance before each visit.
      • Norfolk places data: City of Norfolk Open Data.
      Advanced appearance & developer info

      Shown locally in the footer. Nothing is published until you choose to deploy.

      OPTIONAL ONLINE MODE

      Your choice to connect.

      An account is optional. Your routes, journal, nature notes, and photos stay on this device. This prototype’s online feature only maintains a minimal aggregate profile.

      Connect a Supabase project first

      Add your project URL and publishable/anon key to supabase-config.js, then run the included SQL schema. Until then, everything remains local.
